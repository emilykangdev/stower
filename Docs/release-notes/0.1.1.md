## Stower 0.1.1

### New

- **Send Feedback, right from the app.** A new Feedback sheet lets you tell us
  what's working and what isn't without leaving Stower — your note is delivered
  to the team directly.
- **Privacy front and center.** "Privacy…" is now the first item in the gear
  menu and opens Settings straight to the Privacy tab, so what Stower does and
  doesn't touch is always one click away.

### Fixes

- Contacts permission now reflects your **live** authorization — granting access
  reloads your data once, immediately, instead of waiting on a stale flag.
- Diagnostics consent honors a prior opt-out before anything identifying ever
  runs.

### Under the hood

- Updated demo databases and hardened the feedback delivery path.
