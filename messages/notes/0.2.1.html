Stower 0.2.1

Fixes

  • No more Keychain prompt on launch. Upgrading no longer pops the macOS "Stower wants to use your keychain" dialog that could block the app from opening. Stower now keeps its diagnostics settings in local app storage only, so launch is clean — nothing to allow, nothing to enter.

Under the hood

  • A stable download link. Every release now also publishes a constant-named Stower.dmg alongside the versioned build, so a "download the latest Stower" link always points at the newest release.
  • Diagnostics identity and consent are read only from local app storage; a missing or unreadable record safely resets to defaults instead of reaching for the Keychain.
