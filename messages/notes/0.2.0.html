Stower 0.2.0

New

  • Stower has an app icon. It now shows its real icon in the Dock, Finder, and the installer — no more blank square.
  • Mark a draft as sent. Once you've sent a suggested text, mark it done and it stays resolved instead of reappearing on the board.
  • Tap anywhere on a row. The whole conversation row now opens the composer at the latest message.

Fixes

  • No more repeated permission prompts. New installs arrive via a drag-to-Applications DMG, so Stower runs from /Applications instead of a quarantined copy — your Contacts / Full Disk Access grant sticks instead of re-asking on every launch.
  • Draft edits are more reliable under rapid reloads — in-flight writes are no longer dropped or duplicated, and undo (⌘Z) behaves correctly after a dismiss.

Under the hood

  • Development and released builds are now fully separate ("Stower Test" vs "Stower", distinct identifiers), so a dev build can never disturb the released app's permissions.
